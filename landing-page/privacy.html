Tabi Privacy Policy

Updated: December 19, 2025


Tabi is a Chrome extension that helps you search and switch between browser tabs, bookmarks, common Chrome pages, and optional AI browser actions. This policy explains what information the extension uses and when data leaves your browser.

Information We Collect

  • Tab metadata: Titles and URLs used locally when you invoke Tabi.
  • Bookmark metadata: Bookmark titles and URLs used locally for search results.
  • Local storage: Recent tab IDs used to show your most recently used tabs.
  • AI request context: If you select Ask AI, Tabi sends your prompt and current tab context to the backend so the AI action can be processed.

We do not collect your personal communications, passwords, or payment information.

How We Use Information

  • Search open tabs, bookmarks, and built-in Chrome destinations.
  • Switch to an existing tab or open the selected destination.
  • Show recent tabs when the search box opens.
  • Process explicit Ask AI requests and return structured browser actions.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Local search happens inside Chrome. Ask AI requests are transmitted only when you select the Ask AI result.

Storage & Security

Recent tab IDs remain in your local Chrome storage. Tab and bookmark metadata is not sent to a Tabi backend for ordinary search; it is sent only for explicit Ask AI requests.

Your Choices

  • You can uninstall the extension at any time to stop data collection.
  • You can clear Chrome’s extension storage to remove locally stored recent-tab data.
  • Contact us if you have questions or requests regarding your data.
